Community Housing Associates is moving to a new Employee Portal experience to better track timesheets, leave balances, and the leave request process going forward. You’ve been invited to set up your account.

The portal starts with the pay period that began Sunday, September 13 (September 13–26). Your leave balances have been loaded as of that date.

Your first week

1. Set up your account with the button below.

2. Check your balances on your dashboard — PTO, Sick, and Vacation as of September 13. If a number doesn’t match your records, tell us.

3. Enter any leave you’ve taken since September 13 under Request/Use Leave (dates back to September 13 are accepted through October 8). Sick leave is recorded automatically when your balance covers it; PTO, Vacation, Bereavement, and Jury Duty go to your approver.

4. Review and submit your timesheet for September 13–26 by Friday, October 2. Your regular hours and holidays fill in automatically, and leave appears once it’s recorded.

What to expect

Running alongside our current process. For roughly the first 60 days (about four pay cycles, beginning with the September 13–26 pay period), please use the portal and continue our existing manual process for time and leave.

We’re still improving it. During this period we’ll be refining the system and correcting errors and issues. If something looks off, such as a balance or your hours, please let us know.

Then it becomes official. After that period — or sooner, if no major issues come up — the portal will be the official way to track and report time and leave requests. We’ll confirm the date ahead of time.

Good to know

• Planned time off (PTO or Vacation) can be requested as far ahead as you like. Sick leave can’t be scheduled in advance.

• You’ll get an email when a request is approved or denied. Emails come from the portal — if you don’t see one, check your spam folder.

• Questions about balances, hours, or pay? Contact Carrileen Edwards, Accounting Manager, or use Report an Issue inside the portal.
